4.1 Our legitimate interests

Where the table above refers to legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), our interests are:

  • to operate a football information and networking service that helps players, trainers, owners, clubs and simple users to find accurate, verified information about participants in Greek football competitions;
  • to keep the information accurate and up to date; and
  • to prevent impersonation and fraud, including when someone asks to claim a profile.

We believe this is also in the interest of the participants themselves, who gain visibility and the means to correct their own information.

We display to other users only the name, club, position, statistics and Ratings described in Section 5.

We have weighted these interests against your rights and freedoms and give particular weight to the position of the minors. The safeguards we apply include: access only for users who have completed verification; display limited to the fields in Section 5; no sale of personal data and an easy way to object or to ask for your profile to be removed (see Section 9).

You may object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests (see Section 9).

5. Visibility of your data to other users

Profiles in the App are built from the Public Source. Once a person verifies their identity, that verified user can view the profile information of other participants within the App, and other verified users can view theirs in their profile.

  • Only users who have completed identity verification can access profiles.
  • Information visible to other users is limited to name, club, position, statistics.
  • Contact details and information you add yourself are visible only as per your privacy settings
  • The fact that data are publicly available elsewhere does not mean they are free from data protection rules; we process them in accordance with this Policy.

We rely on our legitimate interests for this visibility. Section 4.1. explains those interests and the safeguards we apply, including access only for verifies users, limited fields and easy removal of your profile.

You can ask us at any time to restrict, correct or remove your profile (see Section 9).

Ratings, statistics and profiling

The App does not simply republish data from the Public Source. We process the data we collect through our own algorithm, which calculates the final statistics, ratings and scores displayed in the App.

(a) Input data: appearances, minutes played, goals, cards and ranking position obtained from the Public Source.

(b) Profiling: because the algorithm evaluates aspects of a person's performance, it constitutes profiling within the meaning of Art. 4(4) GDPR. We rely on our legitimate interests for this processing (see Section 4.1).

(c) No automated decision-making: we do not take decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you solely on the basis of Ratings. Ratings are provided to users as informative indicators only, and we do not use them to decide on your eligibility, selection or contracts.

(d) Limits: Ratings are statistical results of an automated calculation. They reflect only the data and methodology used, may not reflect a person's real abilities, and may change when data or our methodology is updated.

(e) Your rights: you may ask for access to your Ratings, the input data used and general information about how they were produced; ask us to correct inaccurate input data, after which the Rating will be recalculated; object to the profiling; or ask that your Ratings are not displayed (see Section 9).

8. Αποδέκτες των δεδομένων σας

Ενδέχεται να κοινοποιούμε δεδομένα προσωπικού χαρακτήρα σε:

  • Άλλους επαληθευμένους χρήστες της Εφαρμογής, όπως περιγράφεται στην Ενότητα 5·
  • Παρόχους υπηρεσιών που ενεργούν ως εκτελούντες την επεξεργασία για λογαριασμό μας (πάροχοι φιλοξενίας και cloud, επαλήθευσης ταυτότητας, αναλύσεων, εξυπηρέτησης πελατών) βάσει συμβάσεων επεξεργασίας δεδομένων σύμφωνα με το άρθρο 28 GDPR.
  • Επαγγελματίες συμβούλους (δικηγόρους, λογιστές, ελεγκτές)·
  • Δημόσιες αρχές, όταν το απαιτεί ο νόμος ή για την προστασία των δικαιωμάτων μας·
  • Διαδόχους σε περίπτωση συγχώνευσης, εξαγοράς ή αναδιάρθρωσης.

Δεν πωλούμε τα δεδομένα προσωπικού χαρακτήρα σας.
